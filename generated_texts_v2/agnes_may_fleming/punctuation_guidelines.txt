   Follow these patterns for which punctuation typically follows which:

     After quotation mark ("), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (45%), quotation mark (") (24%), period (.) (11%)

     After period (.), the next punctuation is typically: quotation mark (") (43%), comma (,) (39%), period (.) (6%)
     After semicolon (;), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (62%), period (.) (18%), quotation mark (") (10%)
     After question mark (?), the next punctuation is typically: quotation mark (") (78%), comma (,) (11%)
