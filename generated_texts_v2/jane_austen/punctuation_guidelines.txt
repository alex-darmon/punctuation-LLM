1. PUNCTUATION FREQUENCY TARGETS

     comma (,): 46.1%

     quotation mark ("): 15.4%

2. PUNCTUATION SEQUENCING PATTERNS
   Follow these patterns for which punctuation typically follows which:
     After exclamation mark (!), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (40%), quotation mark (") (22%), period (.) (20%)
     After quotation mark ("), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (43%), quotation mark (") (26%), period (.) (16%)
     After comma (,), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (50%), period (.) (28%), semicolon (;) (10%)
     After period (.), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (38%), quotation mark (") (25%), period (.) (23%)
     After semicolon (;), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (59%), period (.) (24%), semicolon (;) (9%)
     After question mark (?), the next punctuation is typically: quotation mark (") (57%), comma (,) (17%), period (.) (12%)

3. SENTENCE LENGTH DISTRIBUTION
   Average sentence length: ~22 words
   Most sentences fall between 8 and 28 words
   Sentence length mix:
     Short (1-10 words):    ~35%
     Medium (11-25 words):  ~37%
     Long (26-50 words):    ~20%
     Very long (50+ words): ~8%

4. PUNCTUATION DENSITY
   Average words between consecutive punctuation marks: ~6.2
   This controls the overall rhythm and density of punctuation.
