=== PUNCTUATION STYLE GUIDELINES FOR H.G. WELLS ===

1. PUNCTUATION FREQUENCY TARGETS
   Match these approximate percentages of total punctuation marks:
     comma (,): 35.6%
     period (.): 31.7%
     quotation mark ("): 24.2%
     exclamation mark (!): 4.0%
     question mark (?): 2.8%
     semicolon (;): 1.2%

2. PUNCTUATION SEQUENCING PATTERNS
   Follow these patterns for which punctuation typically follows which:
     After exclamation mark (!), the next punctuation is typically: quotation mark (") (56%), exclamation mark (!) (14%), period (.) (13%)
     After quotation mark ("), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (39%), period (.) (31%), quotation mark (") (15%)
     After comma (,), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (41%), period (.) (39%), quotation mark (") (15%)
     After period (.), the next punctuation is typically: quotation mark (") (34%), comma (,) (32%), period (.) (28%)
     After semicolon (;), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (46%), period (.) (35%), quotation mark (") (15%)
     After question mark (?), the next punctuation is typically: quotation mark (") (69%), period (.) (12%), question mark (?) (9%)

3. SENTENCE LENGTH DISTRIBUTION
   Average sentence length: ~13 words
   Most sentences fall between 5 and 18 words
   Sentence length mix:
     Short (1-10 words):    ~54%
     Medium (11-25 words):  ~34%
     Long (26-50 words):    ~11%
     Very long (50+ words): ~1%

4. PUNCTUATION DENSITY
   Average words between consecutive punctuation marks: ~4.9
   This controls the overall rhythm and density of punctuation.
