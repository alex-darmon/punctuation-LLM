2. PUNCTUATION SEQUENCING PATTERNS
   Follow these patterns for which punctuation typically follows which:
     After exclamation mark (!), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (41%), period (.) (33%), exclamation mark (!) (19%)
     After comma (,), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (45%), period (.) (36%), semicolon (;) (7%)

     After colon (:), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (77%), period (.) (16%), semicolon (;) (6%)
     After semicolon (;), the next punctuation is typically: comma (,) (52%), period (.) (38%), semicolon (;) (6%)
     After question mark (?), the next punctuation is typically: period (.) (53%), comma (,) (30%), question mark (?) (12%)

3. SENTENCE LENGTH DISTRIBUTION
   Average sentence length: ~8 words
   Most sentences fall between 1 and 10 words
   Sentence length mix:
     Short (1-10 words):    ~76%
     Medium (11-25 words):  ~18%
     Long (26-50 words):    ~6%
     Very long (50+ words): ~1%

4. PUNCTUATION DENSITY
