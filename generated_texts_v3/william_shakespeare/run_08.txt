ELDRIN
Gareth awaits your bidding, swift and keen,
His chosen men, a score of stalwart knights,
Are armed and ready, eager for the fray.
And Captain Bran, a loyal, seasoned hand,
Stands even now without the council doors,
Awaiting leave to enter, and receive
Your Grace's orders. He is a man of few words,
But deep resolve, and knows the forest ways
As well as any tracker in your guard.

VALERIUS
Then let him enter. Bring him forth, good Eldrin.
(ELDRIN exits briefly and returns with CAPTAIN BRAN. BRAN bows deeply)
Welcome, good Bran. We have a task for thee,
A mission of such weight, it bends the back
Of loyalty itself, and tests the soul
Of every man who loves this troubled realm.
My daughter, Isolde, as you well do know,
Has been abducted by a lawless band.
We mean to free her, not by open war,
Which might endanger her most precious life,
But by a stratagem, a cunning trick,
To lull these villains to a false belief
That greed alone can blind a monarch's eye.

BRAN
My lord, my life is yours. Command me now.
To rescue her, or face a hundred deaths,
My sword is ready. Tell me what to do.

VALERIUS
Your spirit, Bran, is like a steady flame
Against the night. Hear then our careful plan.
Gareth, with twenty of our bravest men,
Will ride forth disguised, as wealthy merchants,
Their chests piled high with what appears as gold.
They will approach the brigands' forest lair,
Which Eldrin's scouts have marked with keenest eye,
And offer ransom for the Princess's safe return.
These villains, led by one named Rogan, thrive
On avarice. We shall appeal to that,
And paint our leaden lies with gilded paint.
While they discuss the terms, and count their coin,
Believing they have trapped a golden bird,
Your task, good Bran, will be of subtler might.
You know the forest trails, the hidden paths,
The silent streams that wind through ancient trees.
With ten more chosen men, you shall approach
The brigands' camp from secret, unseen ways,
Like shadows melting through the dappled wood.
Your purpose is to locate where they hold
