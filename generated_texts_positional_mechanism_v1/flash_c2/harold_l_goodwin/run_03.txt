“Don’t blame yourself,” Scotty said, as if reading his mind. “We had to try something.”

The truck engine roared to life, a deep rumble that vibrated through their bodies. The vehicle lurched forward, bouncing roughly over the uneven desert terrain. Rick and Scotty were thrown against the crates, then against each other.

“They’re taking us to the mine shaft,” Rick stated, not really a question.

“I know,” Scotty replied, his voice grim. “But not if we can help it.”

They pressed themselves against the back of the truck, trying to get their bearings in the rocking, swaying darkness. The canvas cover was thick, blocking out any light. It was stifling hot inside, and the smell of dust and canvas filled their nostrils.

“We have to get out of here,” Rick said, his mind racing. “Before we reach that mine.”

“How?” Scotty asked. “This thing is locked tight. And those guys are armed.”
