To pursue one's own vision, especially when it deviates from the expected or the fashionable, requires a certain quiet defiance. She paused, looking out the window for a moment, gathering her thoughts. So yes, Mr. Thorne, I do believe there is a form of courage in that, a steadfastness of spirit that may not involve dragons or daring feats, but is no less significant in its own way."

Mr. Thorne's expression was one of deep satisfaction. "A quiet defiance. A steadfastness of spirit. You have expressed it with remarkable clarity, Miss Croft. Miss Croft felt her heart swell with a mixture of gratitude and something akin to exhilaration. To be counted among those who possessed "such vision" was a compliment she cherished deeply. His words, his belief in her work, felt like a vital encouragement, a validation that went far beyond mere social pleasantries. Lady Ashworth, observing the exchange, smiled. "Indeed, Mr. Thorne. Miss Croft, though still feeling a slight weariness from the intensity of the conversation, managed a polite smile in return. Miss Croft felt her cheeks warm again, a lingering flush that had nothing to do with embarrassment now, and everything to do with a profound sense of connection. "Thank you, Mr. Thorne," she murmured, her voice soft with genuine emotion. "Your perception, your understanding, it has been... it has been truly invaluable." Mrs. Elmsley, with a flurry of farewells and renewed praise for the shortbread, bustled out after him, seemingly invigorated by the refreshments and oblivious to the subtle currents of intensity that had woven through the afternoon.

The drawing room, though still filled with the lingering scent of tea and the soft light of the declining day, felt suddenly quieter, almost empty, despite Lady Ashworth's continued presence. The conversation had ended, but its echoes resonated within her, a symphony of unexpected understanding and profound affirmation. She looked at her paintings, particularly the cottage, seeing them with new eyes, imbued with the rich texture of Mr. Thorne's insightful interpretation. Her solitary world, she realised, had been touched by something extraordinary, something that had the potential to reshape her understanding of herself, her art, and perhaps, even her future. The quiet eloquence of a perceptive stranger. The phrase resonated within Miss Croft like a perfectly struck chord.
