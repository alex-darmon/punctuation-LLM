Alaric, Duke Lyra, and Master Elms remained in the council chamber for hours, drafting official proclamations, arrest warrants for other named conspirators outside the castle walls, and urgent letters to the regional lords.

The proclamations, meticulously penned by Elms, laid bare the full truth of King Theron's murder and the conspiracy that had surrounded it. They confirmed Alaric's rightful claim to the throne, not as regent, but as king, legitimized by the undeniable evidence of his brother's usurpation and murder by others. They called for loyalty from all subjects, promising swift justice for the guilty and unwavering support for the innocent. Most importantly, they outlined the first steps towards renewal: the establishment of a special tribunal to try Kaelen and his co conspirators, the immediate review of all royal decrees issued during Theron's "illness," and the promise of transparent governance.

Messengers, carefully chosen and sworn to secrecy, were dispatched under cover of darkness to carry these vital documents to every corner of the realm. Each messenger carried two identical copies of the proclamation, with instructions to deliver one to the principal lord of their assigned region and the other to the main public square, to be read aloud to the populace. This dual approach was designed to both inform the powerful and reassure the common folk, bypassing any potential attempts at suppression by local complicit authorities.

Alaric also dictated a personal letter to each of the major noble houses, reaffirming his claim, explaining the situation in detail, and subtly assessing their loyalty. These letters, though respectful in tone, carried an unspoken warning: choose your allegiance wisely, for the new reign would tolerate no further treason.

As the pre dawn light began to filter through the high windows of the castle, casting long shadows across the council chamber, Alaric finally leaned back in his chair, a profound weariness settling upon him. His mind, however, remained sharp, invigorated by the immense task before him. He had confronted the immediate threat, secured the capital, and laid the groundwork for justice. But this was merely the first volley in a much longer war for the soul of the kingdom.

Duke Lyra, her own face pale with exhaustion, looked at him with a mixture of awe and concern. "My Lord," she said softly, "you have acted with extraordinary courage and wisdom. The realm owes you a debt it can never repay."

Alaric merely shook his head. "The realm owes King Theron justice, Duke. And it deserves a future free from such perfidy. The debt is to the people, and it is only just beginning to be repaid." He paused, looking out at the city slowly waking to a new day. "We must address the public again, soon. Reassure them, give them hope, but also temper their expectations. This will not be an easy path. The wounds of betrayal run deep."

He thought again of the small boy in the square, whose simple act had unravelled a complex web of lies. That boy, and every citizen like him, represented the true heart of the kingdom. Their trust, their hope, was what Alaric was fighting for.

"Duke Lyra," Alaric continued, a new urgency in his voice, "we need to identify the most immediate priorities for the people. What public works have been neglected? What grievances have festered? What can we do to demonstrate that this new reign is truly for them, not just for the nobility?"

Lyra nodded, already moving into action. "I have a preliminary list, My Lord. Roads, irrigation, grain distribution, addressing the rising banditry in the outlying provinces... Kaelen's corruption touched every aspect of daily life."

"Then we will begin with that. Let the people see that their new King is a man of action, a man dedicated to their well being." He stood, stretching the stiffness from his limbs. "I will need a brief rest, then I will meet with Captain Valerius to review security, and then with Master Elms to discuss the legal preparations for the trials. There is no time for idleness. The work has only just begun."

He walked to the window, gazing out over the sprawling city, its streets still mostly quiet, but soon to burst into the full activity of a new day. The castle, which had been a gilded prison, a silent witness to regicide, now stood as a beacon of hope, its ancient stones infused with a renewed purpose. The air, though heavy with the scent of woodsmoke and damp earth, also carried the faint, invigorating promise of change. The weight of command was immense, a heavy mantle upon his shoulders, but Alaric felt a quiet strength welling within him. His years in exile, honing his mind, body, and spirit, had prepared him for this moment. The path ahead was treacherous, fraught with political intrigue and potential resistance, but his purpose was clear, his resolve unwavering. He would restore the honor of the throne, and with it, the prosperity and peace of the realm. The true reign had begun.
