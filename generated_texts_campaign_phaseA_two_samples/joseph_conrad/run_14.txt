When I first entered that musty, silent hall, where the very air seemed thickened with the dust of centuries and the unspoken tales of forgotten expeditions, I confess a certain tremor seized me. His gaze, which had been fixed afar, now slowly, almost imperceptibly, returned to me, and for a moment, I felt as though he were reading the unspoken questions in my mind, weighing the nascent doubts that flickered behind my own eyes.
