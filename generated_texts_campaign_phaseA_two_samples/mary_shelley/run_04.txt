This tableau, so dear and accustomed, suddenly struck me with a poignant dread. These precious vessels, I thought, how easily they might be shattered; this fleeting joy, how swiftly it could be extinguished. Already the shadow of what is to come, I mused, stretches long before us. Already the subtle corruption, invisible to the common eye, begins its insidious work.
