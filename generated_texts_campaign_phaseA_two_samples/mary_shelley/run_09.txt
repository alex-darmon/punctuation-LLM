These discourses cast a shadow over my burgeoning understanding. A longing for that touch, that simple, unburdened act of connection. A longing to be *seen*, not as an anomaly, but as a kindred spirit, worthy of comfort, worthy of belonging.

The chill I had felt earlier solidified into a pervasive dread. I replayed the scholar's words about "grace" and "charity," the redemptive forces that could counteract human evil.
