A key. I remembered the odd, ornate key I had found in the secret compartment, the one that had felt so strangely significant. It was not for the compartment itself, but a key to something far greater, far more perilous.

A network. Other guardians. This was not just about my mother; it was about an entire hidden world operating beneath the veneer of ordinary society. A world my father had lived in, quietly supporting my mother, for decades.
