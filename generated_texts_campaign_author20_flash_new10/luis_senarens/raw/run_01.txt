It's not a natural phenomenon. It's artificial. And it's building. The energy signature matches the initial phase of the planetary erasure."

"A structure?" Jax mused. "Could it be a temporal device? Thorne's handiwork?"

"It's consistent with his capabilities," Bolt's voice crackled from the comm. He was still monitoring from the future, receiving their filtered data. "Thorne was obsessed with the idea of 'historical pruning,' of altering key events to achieve what he perceived as a more optimal timeline. Erasing an entire planet, however, is an act of unprecedented malice, even for him."

"It appears he succeeded, at least for 700 years," Marta added, her voice heavy. "Now, the question is, how do we stop it from happening this time? We can't simply destroy the device. Any direct intervention could create an even worse paradox, a more unstable timeline."

"No," Jax affirmed, shaking his head slowly. "The Core's guidance is clear. We must not prevent the event by brute force. We must understand it. We must find the historical anchor, the critical nexus point that allows this erasure to occur, and address it directly. The bleed isn't just about the erasure; it's about the reason for the erasure. The intention behind it."

"So, what's the plan, Jax?" Lena asked, looking at him expectantly.

Jax looked out at the vibrant, doomed world. He closed his eyes again, letting the whispers of the Core guide him, sifting through the noise of this altered past. "We observe. We listen. We find the why. And then, we fix it. But we cannot just blow it up. We have to address the root cause, the trigger that led to this atrocity. We look for Thorne. Or whoever he manipulated. We look for the decision maker, the one who gave the command to unwrite Xylos."

"And if it's Thorne himself?" Elias asked, his hand instinctively going to a panel of tool slots near his station.

"Then we confront him," Jax stated, his resolve hardening. "But not with weapons. We confront him with the truth. With the paradox he has created. With the screams of a thousand timelines that are now tearing themselves apart because of his hubris. The Core knows that the greatest weapon against temporal chaos is not force, but understanding. We must reintegrate the true history, not simply rewrite it again."

"Understood," Marta responded, her voice tight with tension but also with renewed determination. "Bolt, Lena, Elias, provide Jax with every piece of contextual data you can. Search for any records, any anomalies, any communication intercepts from this period that could point to Thorne's involvement or the true nature of the erasure. Jax, your intuition is our primary guide. Find the truth. Bring it back."

Jax nodded. He knew this would be the hardest part of the mission. They were not just facing a device or a person; they were facing an idea, a twisted philosophy that sought to control the very essence of existence. And they were racing against a clock that was counting down to the 'Great Silence' once more. The fate of the Kaelen Cluster, and potentially far beyond, hinged on their ability to untangle the knotted threads of a history that was never meant to be forgotten. The silence on Xylos was about to become a symphony of truth, or a cacophony of ultimate destruction. And Jax, the conduit of the Core, was now at its epicenter. His journey had just truly begun.
